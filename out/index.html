Community Guidelines

Server Rules

Keeping XD VERSE SMP safe, friendly, and enjoyable for all players.

01

Respect Players & Staff

Treat everyone with kindness. Harassment, toxicity, and disrespect toward players or staff are not tolerated.

02

Fair Play (No Hacks)

Cheating, X-Ray, hacking, or exploiting bugs are strictly prohibited and will lead to an immediate ban.

03

Family-Friendly Chat

Keep global chat clean and friendly. Avoid profanity, offensive language, or inappropriate topics.

04

No Advertising

Self-promotion, sharing external Discord invite links, or advertising other Minecraft servers is not allowed.

05

Protect Personal Privacy

Respect privacy. Do not share personal details, sensitive real-world info, or private data of anyone.

06

No Chat Spam or Caps

Keep chat clean and easy to read. Avoid repeating messages, spamming, or using excessive ALL-CAPS.

07

Follow Staff Instructions

Always respect and follow guidance from staff members—their decisions keep the server safe and fair.

08

Report Issues Responsibly

Report bugs, player rule violations, or server issues responsibly to staff via Discord tickets rather than public chat.
